## Logo usage

- clear space: Surround the mark with as much free space as possible, away from other graphic elements
- min size: 3 mm height for the mark in all applications
- background rules: Preferred on white; then corporate colour backgrounds; dark blue monochrome on light grey; white negative on dark or non-corporate dark colours; black positive where quality cannot be guaranteed
- misuse: Do not distort, recolour, rotate, apply 3D effects, change the order or proportion of logotype and symbol, tile the mark, place it on busy images or non-approved background colours, or use low-resolution files
