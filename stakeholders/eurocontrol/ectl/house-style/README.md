## Logo usage

- clear space: Guide page 4 draws the boxed logo in a 125 px square with 16 px border radius and a clear zone marked X; no numeric value for X is given.
- min size: 12 mm minimum for best visibility.
- background rules: Works best on a white background and should be used in colour. On white backgrounds the slogan is primary blue in uppercase, with the rounded-corner square suggested alongside the slogan. Dark backgrounds always require the boxed logo: the rounded-corner square with a white background and the slogan in white. The logo with white typeface is for promotional material on dark backgrounds only. A black version is for applications where colours cannot be reproduced.
- misuse: Do not use outlines, shadows or 3D effects; do not change the colours or typography; do not distort or rotate; avoid artistic effects and clutter around the logo; use only official versions; no new or alternative logos.
