## Logo usage

- clear space: A protection area surrounds the logo (drawn for horizontal and vertical versions, no numeric value in the text); no text, image or graphic may enter it.
- min size: unknown
- background rules: Positive version on white is the general rule and mandatory for Commission publications. Textured or graphic backgrounds only if visibility and the protection area are respected, otherwise use a white or flat-colour protection area. On pictures use a solid colour background with sufficient contrast. Contrast between logo and background must meet basic accessibility standards.
- misuse: Modifying, distorting, or separating the logo elements is prohibited; no drop shadows or special effects behind it; for EU-funded projects the EU emblem with funding statement is used, not the Commission logo.
