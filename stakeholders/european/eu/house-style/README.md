## Logo usage

- clear space: unknown
- min size: unknown
- background rules: Preferably on a white background; avoid varied backgrounds or any that do not go with blue. If a coloured background is unavoidable, put a white border around the rectangle, one twenty-fifth of the rectangle height wide. Black only: outline the rectangle in black and print the stars black on white. Blue only: Reflex Blue 100% rectangle with white (negative) stars.
- misuse: Emblem upside down; stars not upright; stars not positioned like the hours on a clock face. The number of stars (12) is fixed. Registering the emblem or an imitation as a trade mark or other IP right is not acceptable.
