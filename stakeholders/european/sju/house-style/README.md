## Logo usage

- clear space: X on every side, where X is the height and width of a square set from the logo drawing (page 8 and 9).
- min size: 25 mm wide with strapline; 15 mm wide without strapline.
- background rules: Monochrome (black or white) logo goes on a solid colour background or a photo with a dark overlay; the black version may be coloured deep blue where graphically suitable. Colour logo uses the gradient.
- misuse: Do not distort, stretch or skew; do not rotate; always use the star; do not resize or move elements; respect clear space; do not change the gradient or colours; do not redraw, always use the latest approved files.
